Title: Traditional Greek Salad (Horiatiki)
Cuisine: Greek
Dietary Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Low-Carb, Keto-Friendly
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
- 4 medium ripe tomatoes, cut into large wedges
- 1 large cucumber (English or Persian), sliced into thick half-moons
- 1 medium green bell pepper, cored and sliced into rings
- 1 small red onion, thinly sliced into half rings
- 1/2 cup (90g) Kalamata olives, pitted
- 200g (7 oz) block of authentic Greek feta cheese (do not crumble; leave as a large slice on top)
- 1 tsp dried Greek oregano
- Salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

Dressing:
- 4 tbsp extra-virgin Greek olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Pinch of salt

Instructions:
1. Wash and dry all vegetables thoroughly.
2. Cut the tomatoes into large, rustic wedges and place in a large shallow salad bowl.
3. Slice the cucumber (no need to peel if using English cucumber) into thick pieces and add to the bowl.
4. Add the sliced green bell pepper rings and red onion.
5. Scatter the Kalamata olives throughout the salad.
6. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the olive oil, red wine vinegar, dried oregano, and a pinch of salt for the dressing.
7. Drizzle the dressing over the vegetables and toss very gently — just enough to coat the tomatoes and cucumbers without breaking anything apart.
8. Place the large block (or thick slab) of feta cheese on top of the salad. Do NOT toss — the feta goes on top.
9. Drizzle a little extra olive oil over the feta.
10. Sprinkle generously with dried oregano and add cracked black pepper over everything.
11. Serve immediately at room temperature. Accompany with crusty bread to soak up the juices.

Tips:
- Authentic horiatiki never has lettuce — it is a "village salad" of chunky vegetables.
- Use the best-quality feta and olive oil you can find; this recipe depends entirely on ingredient quality.
- Let the salad sit for 5 minutes before serving so the tomatoes release their juices into the dressing.
