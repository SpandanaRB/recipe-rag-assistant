Title: Spiced Red Lentil Soup
Cuisine: Middle Eastern / Mediterranean
Dietary Tags: Vegan, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, High-Fiber, Low-Fat
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes
Servings: 6

Ingredients:
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 medium carrot, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
- 1 1/2 cups (300g) red lentils, rinsed and picked over
- 6 cups (1.4 liters) low-sodium vegetable broth
- 1 cup (240ml) water
- 1 tsp salt (more to taste)
- Freshly cracked black pepper
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped

Topping (optional):
- Drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil
- Pinch of smoked paprika
- Lemon wedges
- Crusty bread or pita for serving

Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion, carrot, and celery. Cook for 7–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly golden.
2. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Stir in the cumin, coriander, paprika, turmeric, and cayenne. Toast the spices for 60 seconds, stirring constantly.
4. Add the diced tomatoes with their juices and cook for 2 minutes, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
5. Add the rinsed red lentils, vegetable broth, and water. Stir well to combine.
6. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for 20–25 minutes, until lentils are completely soft and beginning to fall apart.
7. Use an immersion blender to partially blend the soup — pulse 3–4 times to achieve a creamy yet slightly chunky texture. Alternatively, transfer half the soup to a blender, blend smooth, and return to the pot.
8. Season with salt and black pepper. Stir in the fresh lemon juice.
9. If the soup is too thick, add a splash of broth or water to loosen it.
10. Ladle into bowls. Garnish with fresh parsley or cilantro, a drizzle of olive oil, and a pinch of smoked paprika.
11. Serve with lemon wedges and warm crusty bread or pita on the side.
