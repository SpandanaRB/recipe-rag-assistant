Title: Smoky Ground Beef Street Tacos
Cuisine: Mexican
Dietary Tags: Gluten-Free (with corn tortillas), High-Protein, Dairy-Free Option
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 20 minutes
Servings: 4 (3 tacos per person)

Ingredients:
Taco Meat:
- 500g (1.1 lbs) lean ground beef (80/20 blend)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/4 cup (60ml) beef broth or water
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- Salt and black pepper to taste

Toppings:
- 12 small corn tortillas (6-inch), warmed
- 1 cup shredded purple cabbage
- 2 ripe avocados, sliced or guacamole
- 1/2 cup pico de gallo or fresh salsa
- 1/2 cup sour cream (omit for dairy-free)
- 1/2 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend (omit for dairy-free)
- 2 limes, cut into wedges
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Pickled jalapeños, to taste

Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add diced onion and cook for 4–5 minutes until softened and translucent.
2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
3. Add the ground beef to the skillet. Break it up with a wooden spoon and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned — about 7–8 minutes. Drain excess fat if desired.
4. Stir in the cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, coriander, oregano, and cayenne. Cook for 1–2 minutes to toast the spices into the meat.
5. Add the tomato paste and stir to coat the meat evenly. Pour in the beef broth and stir.
6. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer for 5 minutes, until the liquid absorbs and the mixture is saucy but not wet. Season generously with salt and black pepper.
7. Warm the corn tortillas: heat directly over a gas flame for 15–20 seconds per side (char spots are ideal), or warm in a dry skillet over medium heat, or wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave for 45 seconds.
8. Double up the tortillas for each taco for stability.
9. Spoon seasoned beef onto each doubled tortilla.
10. Top with shredded cabbage, avocado slices, pico de gallo, sour cream, shredded cheese, and pickled jalapeños.
11. Finish with a generous squeeze of fresh lime juice and a handful of chopped cilantro.
12. Serve immediately with extra lime wedges on the side.
