Title: Gluten-Free Banana Bread
Cuisine: American
Dietary Tags: Gluten-Free, Vegetarian, Dairy-Free Option
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 60 minutes
Servings: 10 slices

Ingredients:
- 3 very ripe bananas (about 1 1/2 cups mashed)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/3 cup (80ml) melted coconut oil (or unsalted butter, melted)
- 1/3 cup (80ml) pure maple syrup or honey
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 1 3/4 cups (210g) gluten-free all-purpose flour blend (with xanthan gum included)
- 1/2 cup (50g) almond flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp baking powder (gluten-free certified)
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup (85g) chocolate chips or chopped walnuts (optional)
- 1 tbsp brown sugar, for topping (optional)

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan with coconut oil or line with parchment paper.
2. Peel the bananas and place in a large bowl. Mash thoroughly with a fork until almost smooth — a few small lumps are fine and add texture.
3. Whisk in the eggs, melted coconut oil, maple syrup, and vanilla extract until well combined.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the gluten-free flour blend, almond flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
5. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients until just combined — do not overmix, as this will make the bread dense and gummy.
6. Fold in chocolate chips or walnuts if using.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top with a spatula.
8. Sprinkle the top with brown sugar for a slightly caramelized crust, if desired.
9. Bake for 55–65 minutes, until a toothpick or skewer inserted in the center comes out clean. If the top is browning too quickly, tent loosely with aluminum foil after 40 minutes.
10. Allow the bread to cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before slicing — this step is critical for gluten-free bread to set its structure.
11. Slice and serve. Store wrapped at room temperature for up to 3 days or refrigerate for up to a week.
