Title: Smashed Avocado Toast with Poached Egg
Cuisine: Modern Australian / American
Dietary Tags: Vegetarian, Dairy-Free
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 5 minutes
Servings: 2

Ingredients:
- 2 thick slices sourdough bread (or whole-grain bread)
- 2 ripe avocados, halved and pitted
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar (for poaching)
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- Sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 small radish, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp fresh cilantro or microgreens, for garnish
- 1 tsp sesame seeds or everything bagel seasoning
- Pinch of smoked paprika

Instructions:
1. Toast the sourdough slices in a toaster or under the broiler until golden and crisp on both sides.
2. While the bread toasts, bring a medium saucepan of water to a gentle simmer (not a full boil). Add white wine vinegar.
3. Scoop the avocado flesh into a bowl. Add lemon juice, garlic powder, a pinch of red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Smash with a fork to your preferred consistency — leave it slightly chunky for texture.
4. Drizzle in 1 tbsp of olive oil and fold it into the avocado mixture.
5. To poach the eggs: create a gentle whirlpool in the simmering water with a spoon. Crack each egg into a small ramekin first, then slide it gently into the water. Poach for 3 minutes for a runny yolk or 4 minutes for a firmer yolk.
6. Remove eggs with a slotted spoon and drain on a paper towel.
7. Drizzle the remaining olive oil over the toasted bread.
8. Divide the smashed avocado evenly between the two slices, spreading generously.
9. Place a poached egg on top of each toast.
10. Garnish with sliced radish, cilantro or microgreens, sesame seeds, and smoked paprika.
11. Season with extra salt and black pepper. Serve immediately.
