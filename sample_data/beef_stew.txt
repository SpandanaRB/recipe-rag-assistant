Title: Hearty Slow-Cooked Beef Stew
Cuisine: American / European
Dietary Tags: Gluten-Free Option (use GF flour), Dairy-Free, High-Protein
Prep Time: 30 minutes
Cook Time: 2 hours 30 minutes
Servings: 6

Ingredients:
- 1.2 kg (2.6 lbs) beef chuck roast, cut into 1.5-inch cubes, excess fat trimmed
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour (or rice flour for gluten-free), for dredging
- 1 1/2 tsp salt + more to taste
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 large yellow onion, roughly chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cups (480ml) dry red wine (Cabernet Sauvignon or Merlot)
- 3 cups (720ml) low-sodium beef broth
- 2 cups (480ml) water
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary (or 1/2 tsp dried)
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 4 medium Yukon Gold potatoes, quartered
- 3 large carrots, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 3 celery stalks, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 250g (9 oz) cremini mushrooms, halved
- 1 cup (150g) frozen peas, added at the end
- 2 tbsp fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped, for garnish
- Crusty bread for serving

Instructions:
1. Pat the beef cubes completely dry with paper towels — this is essential for a proper sear. Season generously all over with salt and pepper.
2. Toss the seasoned beef in flour, shaking off any excess.
3. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium-high heat until almost smoking.
4. Working in two or three batches (do not overcrowd the pan), sear the beef on all sides for 2–3 minutes per side until deeply browned. Transfer each batch to a plate. This browning (Maillard reaction) is the foundation of the stew's deep, complex flavor — do not skip it.
5. Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining olive oil, then add onion. Cook for 5 minutes, scraping up the browned bits (fond) from the bottom of the pot.
6. Add garlic and tomato paste. Cook, stirring, for 2 minutes until the paste darkens slightly.
7. Pour in the red wine, stirring vigorously to deglaze and release all the fond. Let it bubble and reduce by half, about 5 minutes.
8. Return the seared beef to the pot along with any accumulated juices. Add the beef broth, water, bay leaves, thyme, rosemary, and Worcestershire sauce.
9. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to the lowest setting. Cover tightly and simmer for 1 hour 30 minutes.
10. Add potatoes, carrots, celery, and mushrooms. Stir gently to combine. Continue simmering, covered, for another 45–60 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the beef breaks apart easily when pressed with a fork.
11. Remove and discard bay leaves, thyme, and rosemary sprigs.
12. If the broth seems thin, use a slotted spoon to remove 1 cup of soft vegetables, mash them, and stir back in to naturally thicken the stew.
13. Stir in frozen peas and heat through for 3 minutes.
14. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
15. Serve in deep bowls, garnished generously with fresh parsley and alongside crusty sourdough or country bread.
