Title: Bakery-Style Blueberry Muffins
Cuisine: American
Dietary Tags: Vegetarian
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 22 minutes
Servings: 12 muffins

Ingredients:
- 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup (150g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup (50g) light brown sugar, packed
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1/2 cup (120ml) whole milk
- 1/2 cup (120g) full-fat sour cream (or plain Greek yogurt — adds moisture and tang)
- 1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp lemon zest
- 1 1/2 cups (225g) fresh blueberries (frozen blueberries work too — do not thaw)
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour (for coating the blueberries)

Cinnamon Sugar Topping:
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners or grease well with butter. The high initial temperature creates the characteristic tall, domed bakery muffin top.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, both sugars, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon until well combined.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, sour cream, melted butter, vanilla extract, and lemon zest until smooth.
4. Toss the blueberries with 1 tbsp of flour in a small bowl — this prevents them from sinking to the bottom during baking.
5. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Using a rubber spatula, fold gently until just combined — a few streaks of flour are okay. Overmixing develops gluten and creates tough, dense muffins.
6. Gently fold in the flour-coated blueberries.
7. Divide the batter evenly among the 12 muffin cups, filling each to the very top. This ensures a full, rounded dome.
8. Mix together the cinnamon sugar topping and sprinkle generously over each muffin.
9. Bake at 425°F for 5 minutes (this burst of heat creates the dome), then immediately reduce oven temperature to 375°F (190°C) without opening the door.
10. Continue baking for 14–17 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean and the tops are golden.
11. Let cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.
12. Best served warm on the day of baking. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 3 days.

Tips:
- For extra blueberry flavor, lightly mash 1/4 cup of blueberries and swirl into the batter before adding the whole ones.
- Sour cream is the secret to a tender, moist crumb — do not substitute with milk.
