Title: Pan-Seared Salmon Teriyaki
Cuisine: Japanese
Dietary Tags: Gluten-Free (use tamari), Dairy-Free, High-Protein, High Omega-3
Prep Time: 10 minutes (plus 15 minutes marinating)
Cook Time: 12 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
Salmon:
- 4 salmon fillets (about 180g/6 oz each), skin-on, pin bones removed
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- Salt and white pepper

Teriyaki Sauce:
- 1/4 cup (60ml) low-sodium soy sauce or tamari (for gluten-free)
- 2 tbsp mirin (Japanese sweet rice wine)
- 2 tbsp sake (or dry sherry)
- 1 tbsp honey or light brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp cold water (to thicken sauce)

To Serve:
- 2 cups (370g) short-grain Japanese rice, steamed
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds (white and/or black), toasted
- 3 green onions, thinly sliced on the diagonal
- 1/2 cucumber, thinly sliced
- Pickled ginger (gari), optional
- Steamed broccoli or edamame

Instructions:
1. Prepare the teriyaki sauce: whisk together soy sauce, mirin, sake, honey, sesame oil, ginger, and garlic in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium heat.
2. Mix cornstarch with cold water, then stir into the simmering sauce. Cook for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens to a glossy glaze. Remove from heat and divide in half (half for marinating, half for glazing).
3. Pat the salmon fillets completely dry with paper towels (moisture is the enemy of a good sear).
4. Season lightly with white pepper and a tiny pinch of salt. Brush with half the teriyaki sauce. Marinate for 15 minutes at room temperature (not in the refrigerator).
5. Heat sesame oil in a large oven-safe, non-stick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
6. Place salmon fillets skin-side UP first. Sear for 3–4 minutes without moving until a golden crust forms.
7. Flip to skin-side DOWN. Reduce heat to medium. Brush the top with remaining teriyaki glaze. Cook for a further 3–4 minutes until the skin is crispy and the salmon is cooked to your liking (internal temperature: 125°F/52°C for medium, 145°F/63°C for well-done).
8. Brush with additional teriyaki glaze in the final minute of cooking for an extra lacquered finish.
9. Remove from heat and let rest for 2 minutes.
10. Serve salmon over steamed Japanese rice with sliced cucumber and steamed vegetables alongside.
11. Drizzle any remaining glaze over the top. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds and sliced green onions.
12. Add pickled ginger on the side if desired.
