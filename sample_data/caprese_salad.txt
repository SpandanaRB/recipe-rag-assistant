Title: Classic Caprese Salad (Insalata Caprese)
Cuisine: Italian
Dietary Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Low-Carb, Keto-Friendly
Prep Time: 10 minutes
Cook Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
- 4 large ripe heirloom or vine tomatoes, sliced 1/4-inch thick
- 450g (1 lb) fresh mozzarella (fior di latte or buffalo mozzarella), sliced 1/4-inch thick
- 1 large bunch fresh basil leaves (about 20 large leaves), whole
- 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (use the best quality you can find)
- 2 tbsp aged balsamic vinegar or balsamic glaze
- 1/2 tsp flaky sea salt (such as Maldon or fleur de sel)
- Freshly cracked black pepper
- Optional: 1 tsp Dijon mustard (for a slight emulsification in the dressing)
- Optional: Pinch of sugar to balance very acidic tomatoes

Balsamic Glaze (if making from scratch):
- 1/2 cup (120ml) balsamic vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey or sugar

Instructions:
1. If making balsamic glaze: combine balsamic vinegar and honey in a small saucepan over medium heat. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring occasionally, for 10–12 minutes until reduced by half and coats the back of a spoon. Let cool — it will thicken further as it cools.
2. Remove the mozzarella from its liquid and pat completely dry with paper towels. This prevents the salad from becoming watery.
3. Slice both the tomatoes and mozzarella into even rounds of the same thickness (about 1/4 inch).
4. Arrange the tomato and mozzarella slices in alternating, overlapping layers on a large serving platter in a single row or circular pattern.
5. Tuck whole fresh basil leaves between each alternating slice.
6. Drizzle the extra-virgin olive oil generously over the entire platter.
7. Drizzle the balsamic glaze or aged balsamic vinegar over in a thin stream.
8. Sprinkle flaky sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper over everything.
9. Let the salad rest at room temperature for 5–10 minutes before serving — the tomatoes and mozzarella should be at room temperature, not cold, for the best flavor.
10. Serve immediately. Do not refrigerate once assembled — cold dulls the flavor of both the tomatoes and the mozzarella.

Tips:
- This recipe is entirely dependent on ingredient quality. Use the ripest tomatoes you can find, preferably at peak summer.
- Buffalo mozzarella has a creamier, tangier flavor than cow's milk mozzarella.
- Never use dried basil — fresh basil is non-negotiable for authentic Caprese.
