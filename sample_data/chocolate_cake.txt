Title: Classic Dark Chocolate Cake
Cuisine: American
Dietary Tags: Vegetarian
Prep Time: 25 minutes
Cook Time: 35 minutes
Servings: 12

Ingredients:
- 1 3/4 cups (220g) all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup (75g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 cups (400g) granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk
- 1/2 cup (120ml) vegetable oil
- 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 1 cup (240ml) boiling water
- 1 cup (240ml) strong brewed coffee (enhances chocolate flavor)

Frosting:
- 1 cup (225g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3 cups (360g) powdered sugar, sifted
- 2/3 cup (65g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/3 cup (80ml) heavy cream
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans, then line the bottoms with parchment paper.
2. In a large mixing bowl, sift together the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Whisk to combine evenly.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract until smooth.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and beat with an electric mixer on medium speed for 2 minutes, scraping down the sides of the bowl as needed.
5. Carefully stir in the boiling water and hot coffee — the batter will be thin; this is normal and creates a moist cake.
6. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared pans.
7. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
8. Allow cakes to cool in pans for 10 minutes, then turn out onto wire racks to cool completely before frosting.
9. To make the frosting: beat butter on medium-high speed until pale and fluffy, about 3–4 minutes.
10. Sift in the cocoa powder and powdered sugar in three additions, alternating with the heavy cream, beating well after each addition.
11. Add vanilla extract and a pinch of salt; beat on high for 1–2 minutes until light and spreadable.
12. Place one cake layer on a serving plate. Spread 1 cup of frosting evenly over the top.
13. Place the second layer on top, press gently, and apply a thin crumb coat of frosting all over. Refrigerate for 15 minutes.
14. Apply the remaining frosting to the top and sides, smoothing with an offset spatula.
15. Decorate with chocolate shavings or sprinkles if desired. Slice and serve.
