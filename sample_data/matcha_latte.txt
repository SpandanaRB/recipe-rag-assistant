Title: Creamy Matcha Latte (Hot or Iced)
Cuisine: Japanese
Dietary Tags: Vegan Option (use plant milk), Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free Option, Low-Calorie, Antioxidant-Rich
Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 5 minutes
Servings: 1

Ingredients:
- 1 tsp (2–3g) ceremonial-grade or high-quality culinary-grade matcha powder
- 2 tbsp (30ml) hot water (80°C / 175°F — not boiling, which makes matcha bitter)
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk or plant-based milk (oat milk froths best; almond milk is the lightest)
- 1–2 tsp honey, maple syrup, or agave (adjust to taste; omit for unsweetened)
- Pinch of fine sea salt (optional but rounds out bitterness)
- Optional: 1/4 tsp vanilla extract

For Iced Version:
- Ice cubes
- Cold milk poured over ice
- Shot of matcha poured over top (layered look) or stirred in

Equipment:
- Bamboo matcha whisk (chasen) or a small electric frother
- Small bowl or mug for whisking matcha
- Milk steamer, frother, or small saucepan

Instructions (Hot Latte):
1. Sift the matcha powder through a fine-mesh sieve into a small bowl or the bottom of your mug — sifting removes clumps and ensures a smoother, lump-free latte.
2. Add 2 tbsp of water heated to 80°C (175°F). Using a bamboo matcha whisk or a small electric frother, whisk the matcha vigorously in a rapid "W" or "M" zigzag motion (not circular) for 20–30 seconds until fully dissolved, bright green, and slightly frothy with a fine foam on top.
3. Heat milk in a small saucepan over medium heat until steaming (about 65–70°C / 150–160°F) — do not boil, which destroys the delicate milk proteins needed for foam.
4. Use a milk frother to froth the steamed milk for 20–30 seconds until you have a creamy, velvety microfoam.
5. Add sweetener to the frothed milk and stir gently.
6. Pour the frothed sweetened milk over the whisked matcha in a mug. Hold back the foam with a spoon and pour it on top last for a traditional latte look.
7. Dust lightly with a small pinch of extra matcha powder on top for presentation.

Instructions (Iced Latte):
1. Sift and whisk matcha with hot water as described in steps 1–2 above. Let cool for 1 minute.
2. Fill a tall glass with ice cubes.
3. Pour cold milk over the ice.
4. Slowly pour the whisked matcha over the milk — it will create a beautiful layered green-and-white effect.
5. Add sweetener, stir well to combine, and serve with a wide straw.

Matcha Quality Notes:
- Ceremonial grade is ideal for drinking plain or as a latte — it is sweeter, less bitter, and a brighter green.
- Culinary grade works well for lattes and baked goods at a lower cost.
- Store matcha in an airtight container away from light and heat to preserve its color and antioxidants.
