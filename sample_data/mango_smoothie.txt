Title: Tropical Mango Smoothie
Cuisine: Tropical / American
Dietary Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Refined Sugar-Free
Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 0 minutes
Servings: 2

Ingredients:
- 2 cups (340g) frozen mango chunks (or 2 fresh ripe mangoes, peeled, pitted, and frozen)
- 1 medium ripe banana, peeled and frozen (adds creaminess without dairy)
- 1/2 cup (120ml) canned full-fat coconut milk (shaken well)
- 1/2 cup (120ml) fresh orange juice
- 1/2 cup (120ml) pineapple juice (or additional orange juice)
- 1/2 tsp fresh ginger, grated (optional, adds a warm zing)
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric (optional, anti-inflammatory boost)
- 1 tsp fresh lime juice
- Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional, for a subtle heat)
- Ice cubes, if desired for a thicker texture

Optional Add-ins:
- 1 scoop vanilla plant-based protein powder
- 1 tbsp chia seeds (for extra fiber and omega-3s)
- 1/2 cup spinach or kale (flavor is masked by the mango)

Garnish (optional):
- Fresh mango slices
- Toasted coconut flakes
- Mint sprig

Instructions:
1. If using fresh mangoes, cut the flesh away from the pit, cube it, and freeze for at least 2 hours in advance for the best thick texture.
2. Add the frozen mango chunks and frozen banana to a high-speed blender first (placing liquids on top helps blending).
3. Pour in the coconut milk, orange juice, and pineapple juice.
4. Add grated ginger, turmeric, lime juice, and cayenne if using.
5. Add any optional protein powder, chia seeds, or greens at this stage.
6. Blend on high speed for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy. Scrape down the sides as needed.
7. Taste and adjust: add more lime juice for brightness, more banana for sweetness, or more coconut milk for richness.
8. Add a few ice cubes and blend for 15 more seconds if you prefer a colder, thicker smoothie.
9. Pour into two tall chilled glasses.
10. Garnish with a slice of fresh mango on the rim, a pinch of toasted coconut flakes, and a mint sprig.
11. Serve immediately with a wide straw.
