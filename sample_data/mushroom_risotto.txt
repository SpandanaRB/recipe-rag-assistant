Title: Creamy Wild Mushroom Risotto
Cuisine: Italian
Dietary Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 40 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 cups (300g) Arborio or Carnaroli rice
- 1 oz (28g) dried porcini mushrooms
- 1 cup (240ml) boiling water (for rehydrating porcini)
- 250g (9 oz) mixed fresh mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, oyster), sliced
- 5 cups (1.2 liters) hot vegetable or chicken broth, kept warm
- 1 medium shallot, finely minced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup (120ml) dry white wine (Pinot Grigio or Chardonnay)
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3/4 cup (75g) Parmigiano-Reggiano, freshly grated
- 2 tbsp fresh thyme leaves (or 1 tsp dried)
- 2 tbsp fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Salt and freshly cracked black pepper
- Truffle oil, for finishing (optional)

Instructions:
1. Rehydrate dried porcini mushrooms: place them in a bowl and pour 1 cup of boiling water over them. Let soak for 20 minutes. Lift the mushrooms out (reserving the soaking liquid), chop roughly, and strain the soaking liquid through a fine mesh to remove grit. Add this flavorful liquid to the warm broth.
2. Warm the broth (plus porcini liquid) in a saucepan over low heat; it must be hot when added to the rice.
3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a wide, heavy-bottomed pan over medium-high heat. Add the fresh sliced mushrooms in a single layer (work in batches if needed). Cook without stirring for 3–4 minutes until golden brown. Season with salt and thyme. Remove and set aside.
4. In the same pan, reduce heat to medium. Add remaining olive oil, then cook shallot for 3–4 minutes until soft and translucent. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
5. Add the Arborio rice and stir for 2 minutes until each grain is coated in oil and looks slightly translucent at the edges (toasting the rice is key for texture).
6. Pour in the white wine and stir constantly until fully absorbed — about 2 minutes.
7. Begin adding the warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently (not constantly) and adding the next ladle only when the previous has been absorbed. This process takes 20–25 minutes.
8. After about 15 minutes, stir in the rehydrated porcini mushrooms.
9. Continue adding broth until the rice is al dente — tender but with a slight bite — and the mixture looks loose and creamy (it will continue to thicken when plated).
10. Remove from heat. Add the remaining 2 tbsp cold butter and all the Parmigiano-Reggiano. Stir vigorously — this "mantecatura" step is essential for achieving restaurant-quality creaminess.
11. Fold in the sautéed fresh mushrooms and fresh parsley. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
12. Serve immediately in warm, shallow bowls. Finish with a drizzle of truffle oil and extra Parmigiano if desired.
