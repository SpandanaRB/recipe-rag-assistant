Title: Cauliflower Fried Rice
Cuisine: Asian-Inspired / American
Dietary Tags: Vegan Option, Gluten-Free (use tamari), Low-Carb, Keto-Friendly, Paleo-Friendly
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
- 1 large head of cauliflower (about 900g / 2 lbs), or 4 cups pre-riced cauliflower
- 2 tbsp sesame oil, divided
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (avocado or coconut oil)
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 medium carrots, diced small
- 1 cup (150g) frozen peas
- 1 cup (150g) corn kernels (fresh, frozen, or canned)
- 3 green onions, sliced (white and green parts separated)
- 3 large eggs (omit for vegan)
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp chili garlic sauce or sriracha (adjust to taste)
- Salt and white pepper to taste

Garnish:
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted
- Extra sliced green onions
- Drizzle of chili oil (optional)

Instructions:
1. Rice the cauliflower: remove outer leaves and cut into large florets. Working in batches, pulse florets in a food processor until they resemble coarse rice grains (8–10 pulses). Alternatively, grate on the large holes of a box grater. Spread on a paper towel-lined plate and press gently to remove excess moisture — this step is critical to prevent soggy fried rice.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, rice vinegar, and chili garlic sauce. Set aside.
3. Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil and 1 tbsp sesame oil in a large wok or very wide skillet over high heat until smoking.
4. Add onion and white parts of green onion. Stir-fry for 2 minutes until softened.
5. Add garlic and ginger; stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
6. Add diced carrots and stir-fry for 3 minutes until beginning to soften.
7. Add frozen peas and corn; stir-fry for 1–2 minutes.
8. Push all vegetables to one side of the wok. Add remaining sesame oil to the cleared space. Crack eggs in and scramble quickly with a spatula, breaking into small pieces. When almost set, toss together with the vegetables.
9. Add the riced cauliflower to the wok. Toss everything together over high heat, pressing the cauliflower into the hot surface. Stir-fry for 4–5 minutes, letting it sit undisturbed for 30-second intervals to develop some light char.
10. Pour the soy sauce mixture over everything and toss vigorously for 1–2 minutes until evenly coated and the liquid absorbs.
11. Season with salt and white pepper. Remove from heat.
12. Transfer to a serving dish. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds, sliced green onion tops, and a drizzle of chili oil if desired.
