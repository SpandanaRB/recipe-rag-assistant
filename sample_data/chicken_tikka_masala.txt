Title: Chicken Tikka Masala
Cuisine: Indian
Dietary Tags: Gluten-Free, High-Protein
Prep Time: 30 minutes (plus 2 hours marinating)
Cook Time: 40 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
Chicken Marinade:
- 700g (1.5 lbs) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 cup (240g) full-fat plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

Masala Sauce:
- 2 tbsp ghee or neutral oil
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro and naan/basmati rice for serving

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, combine all marinade ingredients. Add chicken pieces and toss well to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight.
2. Preheat broiler to high. Thread marinated chicken onto skewers (or spread on a foil-lined baking sheet). Broil for 10–12 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and cooked through. Set aside.
3. Heat ghee in a large, heavy-bottomed skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
4. Add the onion and cook, stirring frequently, for 8–10 minutes until deeply golden and caramelized.
5. Add garlic and ginger; cook for 2 minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
6. Add the ground spices (garam masala, cumin, coriander, turmeric, paprika, cayenne). Stir and toast for 60 seconds.
7. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, stirring well to combine with the spices. Season with salt and sugar. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
8. Using an immersion blender, blend the sauce until smooth (optional but recommended for restaurant-style texture).
9. Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour in the heavy cream, stirring to combine. Simmer for 5 minutes.
10. Add the broiled chicken tikka pieces to the sauce and stir gently to coat. Simmer together for 5–7 minutes so the chicken absorbs the sauce flavors.
11. Taste and adjust seasoning. Garnish with fresh cilantro.
12. Serve hot with steamed basmati rice and warm naan bread.
