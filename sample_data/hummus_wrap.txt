Title: Mediterranean Hummus Veggie Wrap
Cuisine: Mediterranean
Dietary Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free, High-Fiber, Plant-Based
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Servings: 2

Ingredients:
Hummus (or use 1/2 cup store-bought):
- 1 can (400g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed (reserve liquid — aquafaba)
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- Salt to taste
- 2–4 tbsp cold water (to adjust consistency)

Wrap:
- 2 large whole-wheat or spinach flour tortillas (30cm/12-inch)
- 1 cup (90g) mixed salad greens or arugula
- 1/2 cup (75g) cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 English cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup (70g) roasted red peppers (jarred or homemade), sliced
- 1/4 red onion, very thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup (35g) Kalamata olives, pitted and halved
- 1/2 avocado, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley or mint, roughly chopped
- 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp za'atar spice blend

Optional Drizzle:
- 1 tbsp tahini + 1 tbsp lemon juice + 1 tsp water, whisked

Instructions:
1. Make hummus: blend chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and cumin in a food processor. With the machine running, drizzle in olive oil and cold water until the hummus is silky smooth. Season generously with salt. Refrigerate if made ahead.
2. If you are roasting your own peppers: place whole peppers on a foil-lined baking sheet under a high broiler, turning occasionally, for 10–12 minutes until charred all over. Transfer to a bowl, cover with plastic wrap for 10 minutes (steam loosens skin), then peel, deseed, and slice.
3. Prep all vegetables and lay them out ready to assemble.
4. Lay a tortilla flat on a clean work surface.
5. Spread 3–4 heaping tablespoons of hummus across the center of the tortilla, leaving a 2-inch border all around.
6. Layer the salad greens in the center over the hummus.
7. Arrange sliced cucumber, cherry tomatoes, roasted red peppers, red onion, olives, and avocado in a line down the center.
8. Sprinkle with smoked paprika, za'atar, and fresh parsley or mint.
9. Drizzle the tahini-lemon sauce over the vegetables if using.
10. Fold the sides of the tortilla in about 1 inch, then roll tightly from the bottom up, keeping tension as you roll to hold everything in place.
11. Slice diagonally in half. Repeat for the second wrap.
12. Serve immediately, or wrap in parchment paper and refrigerate for up to 24 hours for a packed lunch.
