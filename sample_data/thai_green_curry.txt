Title: Thai Green Chicken Curry (Gaeng Keow Wan Gai)
Cuisine: Thai
Dietary Tags: Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, High-Protein
Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 25 minutes
Servings: 4

Ingredients:
Green Curry Paste (or use 3 tbsp store-bought):
- 3–4 green Thai chilies (adjust for heat preference)
- 3 shallots, roughly chopped
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 stalk lemongrass, white part only, sliced
- 1-inch piece galangal or fresh ginger, sliced
- 2 kaffir lime leaves, stems removed
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp shrimp paste (omit for vegetarian/vegan)
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro stems and roots
- 1/2 tsp white pepper

Curry:
- 2 cans (800ml total) full-fat coconut milk, divided
- 3 tbsp green curry paste
- 600g (1.3 lbs) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 200g (7 oz) Thai eggplant (or regular eggplant), quartered
- 1 medium zucchini, sliced
- 1 cup (150g) snap peas or green beans
- 4 kaffir lime leaves, torn (or zest of 1 lime)
- 2 tbsp fish sauce (or soy sauce for vegetarian)
- 1 tbsp palm sugar or light brown sugar
- 1 tsp fresh lime juice
- 1 cup fresh Thai basil leaves (regular basil works)
- 1 red chili, sliced, for garnish
- Jasmine rice, for serving

Instructions:
1. If making fresh paste: combine all paste ingredients in a mortar and pestle or small blender, adding a splash of water if needed, and pound/blend to a smooth paste. Set aside.
2. Open one can of coconut milk. Scoop the thick cream from the top into a large wok or deep skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the cream, stirring, for 2–3 minutes until it begins to bubble and the oil starts to separate.
3. Add the green curry paste to the coconut cream. Stir-fry vigorously for 2–3 minutes until fragrant and the paste darkens slightly.
4. Add the chicken pieces and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until sealed on all sides and coated in the paste.
5. Pour in the remaining coconut milk from the first can and the entire second can. Add the torn kaffir lime leaves.
6. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce to medium and simmer for 10 minutes.
7. Add the eggplant and simmer for 5 minutes until starting to soften.
8. Add the zucchini and snap peas. Cook for a further 4–5 minutes until the vegetables are just tender and the chicken is cooked through (internal temperature 165°F/74°C).
9. Season with fish sauce, palm sugar, and lime juice. Taste and adjust — the curry should balance savory, sweet, sour, and spicy.
10. Remove from heat. Stir in the fresh Thai basil leaves and let them wilt in the residual heat.
11. Serve over steamed jasmine rice in deep bowls.
12. Garnish with sliced red chili and extra Thai basil.

- Saturated Fat: 28g
- Cholesterol: 135mg
- Sodium: 820mg
- Total Carbohydrates: 18g

- Total Sugars: 9g
